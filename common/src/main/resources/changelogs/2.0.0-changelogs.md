## Paint The Universe | v2.0.0 MC-1.21.1

### Important
- Big thanks to @TheArtyrian, @Hecco56, and @aridoitsu for a bunch of help with this update!
- The entire mod has been rewritten from the ground up for 1.21.1; with this rewrite a few issues that aren't directly
stated in this changelog have likely been fixed.
- 1.20.1 worlds with Delicate Dyes updating to 1.21.1 Delicate Dyes may break in some places, update at your own risk!

### Additions
- Added Umber Dye and Lavender Dye, each with their own unique block + item sets
  - Umber is crafted from Red + Brown dyes
  - Lavender is crafted from Purple + White dyes

### Changes
- Simplified the recipe of Sangria Dye from Purple Dye, Magenta Dye, and Black Dye to just Magenta Dye and Black Dye

### Tweaks
- Updated entity colors of Canary and Wasabi dyes
- Updated the texture of Goob Blossoms
- Updated the texture of Roses
- Moved Sangria to after Magenta in the creative inventory

### Removals
- Removed Blueberries and all related items
- Removed most built in integration, this may be a temporary thing however Delicate Dyes isn't a primary focus for me at the moment
  The integration textures will remain in the mod in case anyone would like to make their own addon (integration of my own mods will come on their end)
